## Checklist before product becomes visible

Use this checklist for each product separately.

- [ ] Gross price confirmed.
- [ ] Stock confirmed per size.
- [ ] Product images added.
- [ ] Primary image set.
- [ ] PL and EN descriptions completed.
- [ ] Composition and care instructions completed.
- [ ] Size guide completed.
- [ ] SEO title and description completed.
- [ ] Product checked on mobile.
- [ ] Product approved for launch.
- [ ] Only after approval: set `status=active`.
- [ ] Only after approval: set `isVisible=true`.

## Launch safety notes

- Keep `shopEnabled=false` until explicit launch approval.
- Keep `shopMode=PRE_LAUNCH` until explicit launch approval.
- Do not add launch stock until product data and legal/payment readiness are
  confirmed.
- Do not use placeholder photos or placeholder specs for public launch.
- Do not publish products before final smoke test.
